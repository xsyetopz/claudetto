# Agent Faults Are Recorded As Medical Errors

## Context and Problem Statement

Faults such as sycophancy recur across sessions.

## Considered Options

- Ad hoc notes.
- A named class "medical error" with diagnosis, mechanism, and cure.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: flag faults as medical errors; the user expects the model to fix them better when named.

## Consequences

Each fault goes to `risk-register.md` with its cure.
