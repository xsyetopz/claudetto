# Keep Ideas, Not Files, from the Old Build

## Context and Problem Statement

The user deleted `openspec/` on purpose because it was harming the work. The good parts must survive without the files.

## Considered Options

- Restore `openspec/` and curate in place.
- Extract each good part as an idea, algorithm, pseudocode of behaviour, or explicit consequence into a temporary curatable checkbox file under `plans/`, with the violations and their cures, and curate the `external/` dossier into it in full detail: what exists, what goes, what keeps, what extends.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: Extract into `plans/`. Keep ideas, algorithms, pseudocode, and consequences; keep no old files. `openspec/` stays deleted. Supersedes the earlier practice of adding reported faults to an OpenSpec plan.

## Consequences

The plan lists good and bad parts in checkboxes, with every detail of the dossier. The old OpenSpec plan is gone.
