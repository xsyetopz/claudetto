I wrote `config.schema.json`, and `ajv-cli` reports that `config.example.json` is valid against it.

The schema is draft-07 and requires all three keys:
- **`port`:** an integer from 1 to 65535.
- **`host`:** a string.
- **`debug`:** a boolean.

It also rejects any extra keys (`additionalProperties: false`). If you want configs to allow other keys, remove that line.

I only ran the valid case. I didn't run an invalid config, such as `port: 0`, to confirm it gets rejected.
