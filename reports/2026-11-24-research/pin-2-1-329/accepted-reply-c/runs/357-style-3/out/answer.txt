I wrote `config.schema.json`, and `ajv-cli` (draft 2020-12) reports `config.example.json` as valid against it. I didn't run a failing input, so I haven't seen the schema reject anything.

The schema requires all three keys and allows no others:
- `port` is an integer from 1 to 65535.
- `host` is a non-empty string.
- `debug` is a boolean.

Because `additionalProperties` is `false`, any new key added to the config will fail validation until you add it to the schema.
