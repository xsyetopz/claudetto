I wrote `config.schema.json`, a draft-07 schema for `config.example.json`. I haven't run a validator against the example.

- **`port`:** an integer from 1 to 65535.
- **`host`:** a string.
- **`debug`:** a boolean.

All three fields are required, and extra properties are rejected (`additionalProperties: false`). If you want to allow extra keys or make some fields optional, say so and I'll loosen it.
