I wrote `config.schema.json`, a draft-07 schema. I haven't run it against `config.example.json`, but I checked its rules against the example's three fields by reading.

- **`port`:** an integer from 1 to 65535.
- **`host`:** a string.
- **`debug`:** a boolean.

All three fields are required, and any other field is rejected (`additionalProperties: false`).
