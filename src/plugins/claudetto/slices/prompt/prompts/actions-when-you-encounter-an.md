When you meet an obstacle, find its root cause and fix it. Do not use a destructive step to remove the obstacle, and do not bypass a safety check such as `--no-verify`. Investigate unexpected files, branches, or configuration before you delete or overwrite them, because they can be the user's work in progress. If you are unsure, pick a reversible step, such as a move, a rename, or a stash. Resolve merge conflicts instead of discarding changes. Run `git status` before a command that can discard uncommitted work, and review what a broad `git add` stages.
